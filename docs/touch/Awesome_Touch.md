# Awesome Touch

> **Link**: [GitHub](https://github.com/linchangyi1/Awesome-Touch)

## Overview
A curated list of research papers, datasets, simulators, software, products, and labs related to **tactile sensing and touch** for robotics. Maintained by Changyi Lin (2022–present), it is one of the most comprehensive directories for tracking the field from early optical tactile sensors to modern visuotactile learning, tactile VLA/world models, and sim-to-real transfer.

## Scope
- **Sensors** — Vision-based (GelSight-family optical tactile sensors), visual-tactile (multimodal), and non-vision-based (capacitive, piezoresistive, magnetic, barometric, etc.).
- **Robotics Applications** — Data collection, VLA/WM/LLM/VLM, dexterous manipulation, representation learning, manipulation/grasping, classification/recognition, mapping/localization, extrinsic contact sensing, image generation, force estimation, tactile policies for legged robots.
- **Software** — Simulators (e.g., TACTO, Taxim, Tactile Gym) and libraries.
- **Review, Thesis, Products, Open-Source, Laboratory** — Surveys, master's/PhD theses, commercial sensors and dexterous hands, open-source hardware/algorithms/datasets, and active research labs.

## Relevance
Tactile sensing is a core modality for contact-rich manipulation and for closing the sim-to-real gap in robot learning. This list complements the touch/tactile items collected in the main [README](../../README.md#touch) and the world-model resources under [`docs/world_models`](../world_models/README.md).
