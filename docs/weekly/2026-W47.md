## 🔴 Top story

- **Nov 17 — Hyundai Motor Group takes full ownership of Boston Dynamics.** Hyundai Glovis's quarterly filing confirmed the group bought SoftBank's remaining 9.65% stake, leaving Boston Dynamics wholly held by Chairman Chung Euisun and group affiliates. In the same week, Boston Dynamics expanded its GlobalFoundries collaboration: Spot now patrols fabs in New York, Vermont, Dresden and Singapore (two units logged 90,000 inspections since April), and Atlas begins onsite testing in early 2027. ([Korea Herald](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10940043), [Boston Dynamics](https://bostondynamics.com/news/boston-dynamics-globalfoundries-expand-robotics-collaboration/))

## 🤖 Product launches & breakthroughs

- **Nov 17 — Gemini 4 Hydrogen:** a faster model for general-purpose robotics, folding Gemini Robotics ER 2's embodied reasoning into the Gemini 4 series; scores 58 on Artificial Analysis Robotics 0.1. A robotics plugin for Antigravity shipped alongside. ([Google Developers Blog](https://developers.googleblog.com/gemini-hydrogen-for-robotics/))
- **Nov 16 — Huawei Cloud to open-source CloudRobo-WM1.2**, an Ascend-trained embodied world action model for robots. ([TechNode](https://technode.com/2026/11/16/huawei-cloud-plans-to-open-source-its-ascend-based-world-model-for-robots/))
- **Nov 19 — Dexory launches DexoryView Inspect**, using warehouse robots' routine scans to flag rack damage, hazardous-goods zoning breaches and flue-space violations with photo, timestamp and location. ([Dexory](https://www.dexory.com/insights/dexory-launches-dexoryview-inspect-to-bring-continuous-visibility-to-warehouse-safety))
- **Nov 18 — MIT's 1.29-gram flapping-wing robot** hovered to centimetre accuracy for 30 seconds outside a motion-capture arena carrying its own sensors and computers, then landed on a sunflower. ([MIT News](https://news.mit.edu/2026/featherweight-flying-robot-carries-its-own-sensors-and-computers-1118))
- **Nov 18 — UCSD's fastest soft robot ever built:** a single-material printed six-legged robot running 4.72 m/s on compressed air via a double-expansion pneumatic oscillator — no electronics. ([UC San Diego](https://today.ucsd.edu/story/the-fastest-soft-robot-ever-built-doesnt-need-electronics-to-function))
- **Nov 18 — SCHUNK spin-off becomes TACTILIA Robotics** (Munich): modular robotic hands for humanoids, cobots and mobile manipulators. ([SCHUNK](https://schunk.com/de/en/news/schunk-spin-off-for-humanoid-robot-hands-becomes-tactilia-robotics/41857))
- **Nov 18 — HD Hyundai Robotics ships first 1-ton-class six-axis robot** (HDT1000-33, 0.1 mm accuracy), loading engine blocks at Hankook's R&D HQ in Ansan. ([Korea Herald](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10941164))
- **Nov 17 — 151 humanoid robots set a Guinness World Record**, dancing in sync in Shenzhen (20 EngineAI T800s plus Unitree G1 and AgiBot X2 groups). ([Gasgoo](https://autonews.gasgoo.com/articles/news/151-humanoid-robots-successfully-challenge-guinness-world-record-2123030218822037505))
