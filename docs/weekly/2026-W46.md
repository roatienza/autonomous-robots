## 📄 Research & papers

- **Nov 13 — PEAR: Training Reasoning Models in Simulation to Natively Use Real Robots (MIT Han Lab).** An open-weight 27B reasoning model trained with Robot-Use RL entirely in simulation, without demonstrations, learns to drive real robots natively — no harness, no planners, no skill library. LIBERO: 1.6% → 74.4%, approaching GPT-6 Astra/Sol at max effort; 60% success on a real dual-arm YAM (base model: 0%); LIBERO-Pro instruction transfer 57.4% vs π0.5's 0.75%; no measurable loss on nine reasoning/coding/vision benchmarks. Featured deep dive: [docs/pear/PEAR.md](../pear/PEAR.md). ([arXiv:2611.18562](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.18562), [code](https://github.com/mit-han-lab/pear), [blog](https://mit-han-lab.github.io/pear-blog/))
- **Nov 13 — SuperMimic wins CoRL 2026 Best Paper.** *SuperMimic: Scaling Humanoid Whole-Body Control with One Differentiable World Model* took the award at CoRL in Austin. ([Berkeley AI Research / CoRL](https://blockchain.news/ainews/supermimic-wins-corl-best-paper-for-humanoid-control-model))
- **Nov 9–12 — CoRL 2026, Austin.** The year's robot-learning conference opened with a keynote lineup framing the field's two bets: Fei-Fei Li's world models vs Russ Tedrake's factory robots. NVIDIA used the stage to show **GR00T 2**: 71% success across 50 tasks from one demo each, 95% from five demos (company-reported eval); **Isaac Lab 3.0** adds the Newton physics solver. ([Tech Times](https://www.msn.com/en-us/technology/robotics/corl-2026-robot-learning-conference-opens-monday-li-s-world-models-vs-tedrake-s-factory-robots/ar-AA2h3fyE), [RobotToday](https://robottoday.com/article/robottoday-weekly-november-9-13-2026))
- **Nov 9 — FLEX (MIT + NYU).** An AI-assisted learning platform where robots generalize from conversational instructions, improving motion planning beyond the literal command. ([RobotToday](https://robottoday.com/article/robottoday-weekly-november-9-13-2026))
- **Nov 12 — PRISM (University of Michigan).** A neural network component that learns how forces and motion interact, challenging a common assumption in robot dynamics modeling. ([Tech Xplore](https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/other/prism-helps-robots-move-better-by-learning-how-forces-and-motion-interact/ar-AA2hetkb))
- **Nov 13 — Embodied First Exam.** A new evaluation framework for coding agents that control robots: 1,156 tasks across 35 suites. ([Blockchain News](https://blockchain.news/news/embodied-first-exam-robotics-framework))

## 🧰 Open source

- **Nov 13 — [PEAR](https://github.com/mit-han-lab/pear)** (Apache-2.0, MIT Han Lab). Post-training engine for agentic robotics: veRL + LMCache, vLLM + Ray; runs on RTX 5090/GB300 (Blackwell) or H100/H200 (Hopper); drives YAM and SO-101 arms. Ships LIBERO, LIBERO-Plus, LIBERO-Pro, RoboDojo, RoboLab-120 plus nine general benchmarks via EvalScope. Deep dive: [docs/pear/PEAR.md](../pear/PEAR.md).
- **Nov 11 — [RoboSimStudio](https://www.therobotreport.com/)** (UC Berkeley + NVIDIA). Open-source tooling where teams of AI coding agents write, simulate, and refine readable robot programs — programming industrial robots without task-specific training data. ([The Robot Report](https://robottoday.com/article/robottoday-weekly-november-9-13-2026))
- **Nov 10 — [LingBot-Touch](https://robottoday.com/article/robottoday-weekly-november-9-13-2026)** (Ant Group Robbyant). Open-sourced tactile AI model for robot hands, trained on nearly 5,000 hours of recordings across three tactile sensor types and 26 real-world tasks on two hands. ([RobotToday](https://robottoday.com/article/robottoday-weekly-november-9-13-2026))
- **Nov 11 — Praxis-1 open weights** (Runway). 27-billion-parameter world action model under Apache 2.0; 46.2% on NVIDIA's RoboLab-120 leaderboard. ([RobotToday](https://robottoday.com/article/robottoday-weekly-november-9-13-2026))
- **Nov 11 — Serve Robotics opens its delivery network.** Public API and sandbox: the AV Delivery Developer Portal lets developers and businesses summon Serve's sidewalk delivery robots programmatically. ([RobotToday](https://robottoday.com/article/robottoday-weekly-november-9-13-2026))
